ADD YOUR WEDDING PHOTOS HERE
============================

Drop your image files into this folder (e.g. photo-01.jpg, photo-02.jpg ...).

Then open:  src/components/sections/gallery.tsx
and replace the placeholder gradient tile in <PhotoTile /> with a
Next.js <Image> that points at "/gallery/your-file.jpg".

Until then, the gallery shows elegant placeholder tiles with captions,
so the layout, carousel, and lightbox all work right now.

Tip: keep photos optimized (long edge ~2000px, compressed) for fast loading.
